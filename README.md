# Bill Splitter

A mini project built while learning React. It's a bill-splitting app that lets you split bills among your friends.

## Features

- Add a friend's name to the list.
- Select a friend with whom you want to split the bill.
- Get a form for splitting the bill between you and your friend.
- Reflects the amount you owe your friend.
- Reflects the amount your friend owes you.

## How It Works

1. Add a friend to the friend list if they aren't in it already.
2. Select the friend you want to split the bill with.
3. Upon selecting a friend, you get a Split Bill form.
4. Fill in the bill amount and the amount you choose to pay.
5. Your friend's share of the bill is auto-calculated.
6. Choose who's paying the bill — you or your friend.
7. Click the "Split Bill" button.
8. The result — whether you owe your friend or they owe you — is reflected in their profile in the friends list.
